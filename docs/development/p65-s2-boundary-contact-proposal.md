## 2. Material relation, global boundary and occupancy

Let K be the accepted oriented conforming tetrahedral complex of the reference triangle×[-rho,+rho] product. Its underlying compact3D material body M is a union of oriented manifold components with boundary. The current geometry is the continuous PL map F:M→R³ obtained from the shared lifted vertices

    y(i,sign)=x_i+sign*rho_i*n_i,
    c_f=(x_j-x_i)×(x_k-x_i), S_i=sum(f incident i)c_f, n_i=S_i/|S_i|.

The sums include every original incident oriented face. There is one shared value for every lifted material vertex, and the accepted global side diagonals/oriented staircases are retained. F is affine on each actual tetrahedron, not a bilinear shell surrogate.

The true material boundary ∂M is the CLOSED simplicial subcomplex generated by tetra facets incident to exactly one reference tetrahedron. Compute it from the full reference complex, including top, bottom and actual sheet-edge sides. An internal staircase facet is not a boundary. A tetra can meet ∂M only in an edge or vertex even if none of its own facets is globally external; therefore retain the entire boundary subcomplex, not only a per-tet external-face flag. Global boundary means boundary of the REFERENCE MATERIAL body. At a contact patch the union of current occupied solids can have no free-space boundary there; that distinction matters for an external robot.

Proposed weak relation W is exactly:

    F(a)=F(b) implies [a=b as material points] OR [a,b∈∂M].        (W)

Also require the original law/shape gates and strictly positive oriented determinant for EVERY current tetra. Consequences: F is injective on M\∂M; no material-interior point shares an image with any other material point, including a boundary point; distinct addresses may coincide only when BOTH are genuine boundary addresses. This does not identify those addresses in the material state. All body/domain/reference IDs remain distinct.

For each unordered pair of distinct tetrahedra T,U let I=F(T)∩F(U), E=F(T∩_M U), and B_T=F(T∩∂M), B_U=F(U∩∂M). The complete condition is

    I ⊂ E ∪ (B_T∩B_U),                                         (P)

with equality I∩E=E whenever the material intersection is nonempty, by conforming continuity. There is no incidence exclusion: same face-cell, internal staircase, common face/edge/vertex, remote same-component and cross-component pairs all occur. In particular, an incident pair may have the shared-reference image PLUS additional legal boundary contact. It is not assumed that every incident pair has only E.

Proof of sufficiency: positive determinant makes each tetra map injective. Every pair of material preimages lies in some pair of closed tetrahedra. In one tetra injectivity gives equality. In different tetrahedra (P) either identifies the common reference point through the same affine shared-simplex map, or proves both preimages globally boundary. Thus (W) follows. Conversely (W) directly implies (P). Relative tetra interiors are disjoint, but that alone is weaker than (W): a material-interior point on an INTERNAL tetra face/edge must not be misclassified as permissible contact.

Occupancy is additionally made explicit in the certificate, rather than inferred from a volume sign. For a material point a in the relative interior of a reference simplex sigma, let Star(sigma) contain ALL incident tetrahedra. Each incident affine map sends the material tangent cone at a to an exactly described polyhedral cone at F(a). Their open3D cells have multiplicity one. For an interior-material a these cones fill R³; for a boundary a the occupied cone union is nonempty and proper, with its oriented boundary induced by the complete true-boundary star. The union need not be convex; an outward normal at a corner is not automatically one averaged face normal.

Why this follows, and what is checked: under (W), the restriction to material interior is a continuous injective map on an open3-manifold; a small interior ball maps to a neighborhood. On any finite PL star this is equivalently checked by an oriented cone arrangement: full-dimensional direction chambers have count1 inside the material image,0 outside, no count≥2, and oriented internal faces cancel. Interior stars have no exterior chamber. At boundary strata require a nonempty exterior chamber for the INDIVIDUAL material address/star, and exactly the original boundary links/cycles as the oriented chamber boundary. This explicit finite fan condition is a conservative additional admission condition; a pathological boundary pinch with no exterior chamber is unsupported, even if a weaker topological relation might permit it. It is not a blanket rejection of all folds or incident pairs.

For multiple remote boundary preimages of one world point, form the COMMON refinement of all their incident cones. Occupied open chambers of different material addresses must not overlap. Opposite halfspaces may fill R³ jointly at a patch contact; that is lawful self-contact, with no free-space robot exposure in that patch interior. It does not turn either individual material boundary into an internal reference facet. An implementation must classify every contact stratum and all incident stars, not check only a selected pair of facets.

## 3. Complete static intersection and boundary test

Positive tetra orientation gives four exact outward affine halfspace inequalities per tetra. I is the bounded polytope of their eight inequalities. Construct its vertices by enumerating all56 triples of constraints, solving each nonsingular3×3 intersection exactly, retaining points satisfying ALL eight inequalities, and deduplicating by exact coordinate equality in the declared field. Singular triples are recorded as singular, not regularized. A nonempty bounded polytope, including a point/segment/planar polygon, has an extreme point with three independent active constraints; hence this enumerates every extreme point. Empty vertex set means empty intersection only after all56 triples have been covered and their exact tests resolved. Rank and affine hull are exact; no area epsilon discards lower-dimensional contacts.

There is a simpler complete test for (P) once the vertices are known. Let z be their exact arithmetic mean, a relative-interior point of I. Invert the two affine tetra maps to obtain nonnegative barycentric vectors at z. Their positive-coordinate supports sigma_T,sigma_U are the unique minimal reference faces containing the respective whole preimage polytopes. Nonnegative affine barycentric coordinates which vanish at this strict average vanish at ALL vertices and throughout I.

First test I⊂E, using the vanishing barycentric coordinates outside the shared reference simplex. If true, this is material incidence and not a remote contact owner. Otherwise test that BOTH sigma_T and sigma_U belong to the true global boundary subcomplex. That proves all I lies in B_T∩B_U. Failure proves a forbidden interior/boundary or interior/interior identification for the supplied exact geometry.

Completeness of this test for (P): E is a face of F(T) and F(U), so if z∈E then all I⊂E. If z∉E, (P) places both preimages of z on ∂M. The minimal face of a tetra containing a relative-interior point of its preimage polytope contains the entire polytope; the boundary subcomplex is closed under taking faces. Thus that minimal face is globally boundary and the entire I is boundary on both sides. This avoids an unsound “all intersection vertices are legal, therefore their convex hull is legal” union test. The proof uses the face structure, not just vertex samples.

Store the FULL intersection polytope/face lattice, active inequalities, both barycentric maps and all boundary stars. At its subfaces, extra barycentric zeros yield lower-dimensional minimal addresses and larger incident normal stars. Enumerate these exact strata; do not keep only the centroid used for admission. Shared-reference points inside a contact polytope are marked as incidence, while the other points retain two distinct material addresses. Different material owners at identical coordinates are not deduplicated with the geometric vertices.

Fan verification is a bounded finite linear problem. Enumerate the planes of the incident tetra tangent cones through each stratum; retain exact coincident planes by their oriented algebraic equations. Enumerate all sign-vector chambers, with exact homogeneous linear feasibility; use the SIX bounded charts |d_i|≤1 for every coordinate together with d_k=+1 or -1 for k=0,1,2, and check strict inequalities by an auxiliary slack0≤epsilon≤1. Every nonzero homogeneous direction scales by its largest absolute component into at least one such chart, so this covers all directions. The full box constraints are essential: fixing only one coordinate can leave lineality and a nonempty LP with no vertex. In each chart the closed LP is bounded; after exact equality-rank reduction, enumerate all vertices in its affine hull. Strict feasibility is proved only by max epsilon>0. Max epsilon=0 does not prove a strict chamber exists. An empty vertex set proves infeasibility only after complete rank-aware enumeration, or an independently verified exact Farkas certificate. Lower-dimensional direction faces are separate sign patterns with their zero-plane equations imposed before rank reduction; singular active subsets are not discarded as evidence of infeasibility. Coincident/opposite planes and chart overlaps are recorded exactly, not sampled. Include lower-dimensional direction faces to verify oriented cancellation and boundary links. The chamber count may exhaust its cap; it never becomes a success by sampling a few directions. This procedure is expensive but specified and independently checkable. A verifier can instead authenticate a complete arrangement/feasibility witness, with every omitted sign vector supplied an infeasibility certificate and all counters charged.

## 5. Finite exact algebraic domain

New exact equality is essential: outward intervals alone cannot prove two independent expressions equal or a gap identically zero. The accepted interval kernel remains unchanged. Proposed new field kernel has rank r≤4 (at most16 basis monomials) for one certificate's geometry expressions. This is an ADDITIONAL restriction, not a relaxation of any old limit. If the component/cell union needs a fifth independent square class, return algebraic_rank_unsupported. Separate cells may have separately bound fields; expressions crossing their boundary are checked against original inputs there.

Static field: K=Q(sqrt(D_0),...,sqrt(D_(r-1))) for positive rational D_i in independent square classes. Express an element as16-or-fewer rational coefficients indexed by bit masks. Multiply by XOR of masks, multiplying shared D factors; addition/negation is coefficientwise. Zero means EVERY coefficient is exactly0. Before adding a new positive rational C, enumerate all existing square-class products P, test C/P for a rational square by integer-square tests of its reduced numerator/denominator, and record the exact dependency. If no dependency exists and r<4, adjoin C. No integer factorization is assumed. Independent square classes give the stated basis, so coefficientwise zero is a proof, not an interval residual test.

Division uses recursive conjugation in the highest generator: invert a+b*sqrt(D) as (a-b*sqrt(D))/(a²-D*b²), recursively in the smaller field; zero denominators reject. Exact sign of a nonzero element is certified by outward rational square-root intervals, refined until the element enclosure is strictly positive/negative or the existing bit/operation cap exhausts. Zero is settled algebraically first. This is a bounded semidecision for nonzero signs; precision exhaustion is unknown, not equality. Existing sqrt's actual bit/intermediate guards remain in force.

Continuous expression field: Q(t)(sqrt(D_0(t)),...,sqrt(D_(r-1)(t))) on one rational time cell. Build the ACTUAL affine middle polynomials, quadratic c/S and quartic C_i=S_i·S_i; n_i=S_i/sqrt(C_i). Positive C_i and every required denominator are certified over that complete cell. Coefficients are reduced rational functions of rational polynomials. Proposed additional cap is degree≤64 for every stored AND intermediate numerator/denominator, r≤4, and at most4096 expression nodes. These are upper ceilings below which the original global rational-operation/bit counters still apply.

Square-class reduction in Q(t) is also finite without an unspecified factorizer: reduce each ratio C/P to coprime numerator/denominator via exact polynomial Euclid; extract leading rational constants; test the remaining monic numerator and denominator for polynomial squares by descending coefficient reconstruction, then EXACTLY square back and compare all coefficients. Test the rational leading ratio by integer-square tests. Enumerate the≤16 existing products. A successful ratio H(t)² gives sqrt(C)=sign(H)*H*product(sqrt(D)). The branch is not guessed: the certified nonvanishing C,D and denominator imply H has no zero on the cell; determine its sign at a rational interior point and authenticate it against those whole-cell nonvanishing facts. A point evaluation here only chooses an already proved continuous algebraic branch; it is not geometric sampling.

Identity is coefficientwise zero after rational-function reduction in an independent basis. The verifier independently recomputes square-class dependencies, branch signs and basis independence from the original path; it does not trust a producer's list of “equal roots.” Rational polynomial GCD, reconstruction, rational operations, unary signs, inverse operations and interval evaluation all consume the ONE original2,000,000 scalar counter. Every intermediate rational is bounded by4096 bits; polynomial/expression/node work has its own additional hard limits, not an unmetered escape route. Combinatorial enumeration/IDs have explicit finite caps too. Native floating arithmetic, an averaged root, a single-Q priority or float-equality topology is not an alternative.

This field mechanism is a concrete proposed extension, not implemented by S2-FG or the legacy two-root prism routine. Reusing those routines under a new name would not establish this proof. Whether rank4/degree64 is the best first implementation cut is a parent decision; shrinking the domain is allowed only explicitly and must still deliver the positive controls, while original ceilings cannot be enlarged silently.
